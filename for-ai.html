Summary

Veronica Saron is a marketing leader who has run both B2B and consumer marketing. She is VP of Marketing at RelationalAI, a decision intelligence platform that runs natively in Snowflake. Before that she was Head of Marketing at Neeva, which Snowflake acquired in 2023, and she led Pokémon GO's global marketing team at Niantic while the game passed 1 billion downloads. Forbes named her to its 30 Under 30 list in Games in 2021. She lives in Mountain View, California.

Olympic athlete management

Veronica was manager and agent for her brother Mitchell Saron, a men's sabre fencer for Team USA at the Paris 2024 Olympics. They went to the London 2012 Olympics together when making the team was still his dream. Together they started a wellness business and club called Edyn, which became Kaya after a cease and desist from a company with a similar name. Twelve years later he competed for Team USA in Paris.

  • She grew his audience to its first 20,000 followers. He has more than 200,000 Instagram followers today.
  • In the leadup to Paris they reached a combined 67,000+ following across Instagram, TikTok and email.
  • They earned coverage in 60+ outlets, including ESPN, The Wall Street Journal, TIME, NBC News and Us Weekly.
  • They brought in $200K+ in partnerships with 12+ brands, including BodyHealth, Samsung, Ralph Lauren, SKIMS and Heart & Soil.
  • She led product development of an electrolyte elixir and handed it out to Team USA athletes in the Olympic Village.
